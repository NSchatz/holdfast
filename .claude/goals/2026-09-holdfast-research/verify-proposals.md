# Adversarial verification of P1-P6 (2026-09-29)

Method. For each proposal the `## Recommendation` was read first, and the claims it actually rests on were picked: rows of its "Claims re-verified" table marked confirmed or corrected, plus factual claims made inside the Recommendation. Each claim was then attacked with primary sources only, read 2026-09-29: holdfast code on `main` at `94b86fc` (identical in code to `30d245f` except the files `git diff --name-only 30d245f 94b86fc` lists: `CLAUDE.md`, `Makefile`, two workflows, `docs/secrets.md`, `internal/server/server_test.go`, the identity-scan scripts and the goal 1 ledger), the brief `.claude/goals/2026-09-holdfast.md`, the read-only umbrella clone at `4104b5d9`, pull-request metadata through `gh` (read only), the pinned `ffmpeg` binary's own help output, upstream source at the cited revisions or tags, the RFCs, the vendor licence texts, Debian's package pages and the projects' official documentation. A secondary source was used only as a lead. P1 and P6 were checked directly; P2-P5 were checked by four parallel sub-verifiers under the same rules. Every correction they report against holdfast code was re-read in `/workspace` before it went into this report, and a sample of their upstream evidence was re-fetched (dovi_tool `exporter.rs` at 2.3.4, BtbN `50-libva.sh` at the pinned autobuild tag, FFmpeg `vaapi_encode_h265.c` at `5d4d3bdc61`, the pinned binary's encoder help); rows marked "re-read by the lead verifier" are those. Two process deviations: the four sub-verifiers ran at once, above T40's cap of two agents (the cap was not read before they started), and the P2 sub-verifier ran two sub-second CPU test encodes (a 2-frame 128x128 lavfi clip) without the heavy lock. Verdicts: stands = a primary source says so; corrected = right in substance, but a detail the proposal states is wrong; refuted = a primary source contradicts it; unverifiable = no primary source was reached in the time budget. No GPU, account, token or paid service was used, and the sub-verifiers ran dovi_tool and ffmpeg only on copies in scratch space.
